Oklahoma Corvette club · Not-For-Profit · 501(c)(3)

Corvette people, good company, and a hand for those in need.

We are a club of Corvette owners who take part in events and social activities together, and who are willing to support those in need, assist with charitable events and support our local schools and other community activities.

Our purpose

Why the club exists

“The purpose of the Heart of Oklahoma Corvette Club (HOCC) is to encourage participation of the club members in events and social activities that promote HOCC and its membership. The members of HOCC are willing to support those in need, assist with charitable events and support our local schools and other community activities.”
